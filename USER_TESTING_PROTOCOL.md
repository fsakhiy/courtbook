# CourtBook HiFi Prototype - User Testing Protocol

## Executive Summary
This document outlines the complete protocol for conducting moderated user testing with 5-8 participants to evaluate the CourtBook high-fidelity interactive prototype with emphasis on privacy understanding and usability.

---

## 1. Testing Overview

### Objectives
1. **Task Completion**: Measure ability to complete core workflows
2. **Privacy Understanding**: Assess comprehension of data practices
3. **Trust Assessment**: Evaluate confidence in data privacy
4. **Usability**: Identify friction points and confusion
5. **Interaction Design**: Validate effectiveness of feedback mechanisms

### Scope
- **Participants**: 5-8 users (badminton enthusiasts, aged 20-45)
- **Duration**: 45-60 minutes per session
- **Method**: Moderated in-person or remote testing
- **Tools**: Screen recording, task observer notes, post-test survey

### Success Metrics
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Task Completion Rate | 80%+ |
| Privacy Understanding Quiz | 70%+ correct |
| Trust Score | 7.5+/10 |
| System Usability Scale | 70+ |
| Time on Task (Browse & Book) | <10 minutes |

---

## 2. Participant Recruitment

### Target Demographics
- **Age**: 20-45 years old
- **Experience**: Regular badminton players (2+ times/month)
- **Tech Comfort**: Comfortable with mobile/web apps
- **Location**: Jakarta metro area
- **Language**: Fluent in Indonesian or English

### Recruitment Criteria (Inclusion/Exclusion)
**Include:**
- Active badminton players
- Haven't used CourtBook before
- Willing to provide feedback
- Can attend 1-hour session

**Exclude:**
- Employees of competitor booking services
- Under 18 or over 50
- Heavy privacy-conscious users (may have extreme expectations)

### Recruitment Methods
- Social media badminton groups
- University sports clubs
- Badminton center partnerships
- Snowball sampling (referrals)

## 3. Testing Tasks

### Task 1: Explore Privacy Controls (5 minutes)
**Objective**: Measure discoverability and understanding of privacy features

**Instruction**: "Before booking, explore the privacy settings. Tell me what data this platform collects and why. Don't rush—take your time to understand."

**Observations to Note**:
- Time spent exploring
- Which sections did they visit?
- Did they find consent toggles?
- Did they read privacy badge explanations?
- Did they understand data minimization mode?

**Success Criteria**: Can articulate ≥3 data minimization features

---

### Task 2: Browse Courts with Filters (4 minutes)
**Objective**: Evaluate search/filter usability and privacy badge awareness

**Instruction**: "You want to book an indoor badminton court in South Jakarta for around Rp 100,000/hour. Find suitable options. Pay attention to what each court's privacy level means."

**Observations to Note**:
- Did filters work intuitively?
- Did they notice privacy badges?
- Did they read privacy badge tooltips?
- Did they sort by any criteria?
- Any confusion with floor materials or facilities?

**Success Criteria**: Finds ≥2 appropriate courts in <4 minutes

---

### Task 3: Complete Booking Flow (8 minutes)
**Objective**: Assess booking workflow and consent comprehension

**Instruction**: "Now book 'Champion Badminton Club' for Saturday May 9 at 22:00 for 1 hour with 4 players. Complete the entire booking process. Pay attention to what information you're asked for and what permissions you're giving."

**Observations to Note**:
- Ease of navigating the booking form
- Understanding of consent checkboxes
- Did they select minimal data mode?
- Time to complete each step
- Any fields they found unnecessary?
- Did they read terms/privacy disclosures?

**Success Criteria**: Completes booking with ≥2 consent decisions understood

---

### Task 4: Review and Manage Booking (3 minutes)
**Objective**: Evaluate post-booking clarity and booking management features

**Instruction**: "Now go to 'My Bookings' and review what you just booked. Show me how you would cancel this booking if needed."

**Observations to Note**:
- Can they find my bookings section?
- Is booking status clear?
- Do they understand cancellation implications?
- Did they notice confirmation details?

**Success Criteria**: Can view and understand booking details; knows how to cancel

---

### Task 5: Privacy Quiz (5 minutes)
**Objective**: Measure actual retention of privacy information

**After all tasks**, ask these questions without allowing app reference:

**Questions**:
1. "What's the minimum information CourtBook requires to complete a booking?" (Answer: Name, email, phone)
2. "How long does the platform keep your booking data?" (Answer: 2 years after last booking)
3. "What does 'Minimal Data' badge mean?" (Answer: Only essential info collected)
4. "Can you delete your data? How?" (Answer: Yes, through Privacy Settings → Delete)
5. "Does CourtBook sell your data to third parties?" (Answer: No, never)
6. "What type of cookies can you control?" (Answer: Analytics, Marketing, not Essential)

**Scoring**: 1 point per correct answer (max 6 points = 100%)
**Target**: ≥70% correct (4/6 questions)

## 5. Testing Session Structure

### Pre-Session (5 minutes)
- Welcome & thank you
- Explain purpose (improve UX, especially privacy)
- Get informed consent & permission to record
- Explain think-aloud protocol
- Demographic questions

### Main Testing (30-35 minutes)
- Task 1: Privacy Exploration
- Task 2: Browse Courts
- Task 3: Book Court
- Task 4: Review Booking
- Task 5: Privacy Quiz

### Post-Testing (10 minutes)
- Usability survey
- Trust questions
- Open-ended feedback
- Discuss key observations
- Thank you & incentive

**Total Duration**: 45-60 minutes

## 6. Facilitator Guidelines

### Best Practices
- **Stay neutral**: Don't suggest solutions or confirm they're doing it right
- **Encourage thinking aloud**: "What are you looking for?" "Why did you click that?"
- **Don't interrupt**: Let them struggle for 10-15 seconds before offering help
- **Prompt gently**: "Can you tell me what you're doing?" not "Why didn't you click X?"
- **Note emotions**: Frustration, confusion, delight all matter
- **Probe deeply**: Follow up vague answers with "Can you tell me more?"

### If They Get Stuck
- After 30 seconds: "What would help you right now?"
- After 60 seconds: Gentle hint: "Have you tried [area]?"
- After 90 seconds: Offer help: "Would it help if I pointed you toward..."
- Skip if necessary: "Let's move on" (note as task failure)

### Recording & Notes
- Screen + audio recording (with consent)
- Live note-taking on prepared observation form
- Timestamp key moments
- Note emotional reactions
- Capture exact wording of user's comments

---

## 7. Data Analysis

### Quantitative Metrics
**Task Completion**:
- Track: Completed / Attempted × 100
- Target: ≥80% for each task

**Time on Task**:
- Track: Minutes per task
- Benchmark: Browse <4 min, Book <8 min

**Privacy Understanding**:
- Quiz score: # correct / 6 × 100
- Target: ≥70%

**Trust Scores**:
- Average 0-10 trust ratings
- Target: ≥7.5

**SUS Score**:
- Calculate per standard SUS formula
- Target: ≥70 (industry standard for "good")

### Qualitative Analysis
**Thematic Coding**:
1. **Privacy Concerns**: What specific data worried participants?
2. **Pain Points**: Where did they struggle?
3. **Confusion Areas**: What wasn't clear?
4. **Delight**: What did they love?
5. **Trust Triggers**: What made them confident?

**Affinity Mapping**:
- Group similar feedback
- Identify patterns across 5-8 users
- Prioritize by frequency + severity

## 10. Ethical Considerations

### Consent
- Clear explanation of purpose and recording
- Voluntary participation
- Right to withdraw at any time
- Clear data usage policy for testing data

### Privacy of Test Data
- All recordings deleted after 30 days
- Anonymized quotes in reports
- No personally identifiable information shared
- Test data not used for any purpose other than UX improvement

### Accessibility
- Offer session times convenient for participants
- Provide remote option for testing
- Clear communication about expectations
- Respectful treatment of all feedback
